# Campaign front renders

These seven transparent PNGs were produced with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool from each existing `front.png` as the garment reference. Transparent WebP derivatives are served in the product detail stage to reduce image transfer. Original rail and angle images remain in place.

## Prompt set

Each request used this common direction: "Create a premium CGI fashion campaign product render from this exact reference garment. Preserve its silhouette, color, textile, trims, and defining construction details. Show the complete garment front facing, isolated and floating naturally, without a hanger, mannequin, model, or props. Use sculptural soft key light, a restrained edge light, tactile fabric detail, luxury editorial art direction, and a photorealistic 3D render. Center the entire garment with breathing room on a transparent background. No text, watermark, or added objects."

The subject details were specified separately for each render:
